The food I chose for my essay

Cheese Cake


Here's an interesting fact about cheese cake, the first recorded piece of history including cheese cake was during the original Olympics in Ancient Greece. It was served to the athletes participating in the event, though it is very different from a modern cheese cake. My favorite style of cheese cake is probably the New York style of cheese cake, which is known most for how creamy and delicious it is.

Cheese Cake has a long history, while starting in the Olympics it served many purposes. Cheese Cake, an alternative sacrifice, was used as a non-violent way to make sacrifices to the Ancient Greek gods and goddesses. The most prominent example would be the sacrifices to Artemis during Mounychion, April or May, during a full moon. It was also used by the Romans in a similar way to their own deities, though ingredients varied. Though the start of modern cheese cake happened in England 1265, which started the use of soft cheese according to account books by the Countess of Leicester.

Cheese Cake is also very important to me as an individual, I've had a lot of memories involving the dish. I remember the first time I moved in with my dad, he had gotten one for me after I started doing better in school and doing more around the house. I'd say my favorite part of the dish is just how creamy it is, easy to chew on and the flavor is really smooth and sweet. Sure it's not the healthiest of course being a pastry with cream cheese and a lot of sugar, but it holds some memories. I have honestly brought some for myself on a few occasions, sadly I've never been to the Cheese Cake Factory but one day I do want to try it.

Here's my food narrative: "I stood in the middle of a cold aisle, which was illuminated by a single warm light, and stared down. There was a plastic carrier with a sticker and shine on it, Cheese Cake. I caved and brought it, after all I deserved something after all the stress I had this week. Once I returned I had stored away everything else I brought that day. All that was left was me and that cake. I opened it, of course, and decided to grab a slice without a plate or knife, I wanted to eat it swiftly. The slice of the cheesecake was also a slice of my childhood. I remember clearly my mother and her work. Every now and then she'd buy cheese cake for me and her to share as we sat down. I ate the entire thing in one bite, the contrast textures felt nice. A mix of the crumbly pie crust and the smooth cream cheese, I ate it much too fast to savor it. This time I grabbed a plate and knife, the knife cutting through cleanly, I plated the slice and got a fork. Sitting down I cut a piece of the slice and carried it to my mouth as I chewed slowly. The creamy and sweet taste combined with the crust was exquisite. I continued eating while the cake was still cold, catching the smooth smell with the pleasing scent of a baked pastry. Once I finished my second slice I had put the cake away and waited for when I felt stressed again."

Cheese Cake is a wonderful dish, there are still a lot more reasons why I like cheese cake, it's a good dessert. Its rich history that tracks back so far and wide turning into different styles. The past I have with it and all the memories, good and bad I've associated with it. The way it inspires me to write in detail, to help train my imagination. It's a dish that's important to me and that I enjoy.
